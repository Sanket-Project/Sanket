# Company-level prospect research: Apparel (India), pass 1
Author: Research Analyst (Axon, Research & Insights), model DeepSeek Flash, run 2026-10-09.
Method: Composio's Exa-backed web search (16 parallel discovery queries), read-only. No emails or messages sent, no CRM or HubSpot writes, no accounts or forms. Company-level only; personal profiles that surfaced were discarded.
Scope caveats: the analyst hit a 20-step limit before writing a final answer, and had read only the Apparel part of the saved results. Hardware was NOT read in this pass (see file 07 for the follow-up). Everything below is "seen in a search result", not independently verified. Sizes are as reported by the sources and often conflict.

## Candidates (best fit first)
1. Shree Mahalasa and Co (Nashik, Maharashtra). Multi-brand innerwear, athleisure, sportswear, leisurewear; founded 2009; stores in Nashik, Nagpur, Surat, Jalgaon, Sangamner. Why a fit: multi-brand, multi-state store network (35+ stores per one source), size and pack SKUs allocated per store. Size: 111 employees (LinkedIn/Exa org profile) vs "200+ across 35+ stores" (second source), conflicting, treat as 111-200+. Role: Head of Retail Operations, or Supply Chain and Inventory Manager. Source: https://linkedin.com/company/shree-mahalasa-and-co-s
2. Girisons (Pune; offices/stores in Nagpur and Mumbai). Retail apparel and fashion across cities. Why: store-wise demand differs by city, no evidence of an in-house planning team. Size: unverified. Role: Head of Retail / Supply Chain Manager. Source: https://linkedin.com/company/girisons
3. Adonis Group (Karnataka). Branded apparel distribution AND retail. Why: sell-in forecasting and store replenishment in one company. Size: unverified. Role: Head of Supply Chain / Distribution Planning Manager. Source: https://www.adonisgroup.in/
4. Vastramay (city unverified). Appeared because it was hiring for SKU/style-level demand forecasting, replenishment, aged-stock liquidation, size/colour ratio work. Strongest "already has this problem" evidence. Size unverified. Role: Merchandise / Demand Planning Manager. Source: https://in.findjob24h.com/merchandise-planner-job326915
5. Jainam Overseas Private Limited (unverified location). Apparel (export/wholesale-retail per hiring post); publicly hiring merchandise/demand planning. Size unverified. Role: Merchandise Planning Manager / Demand Planning Lead. Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jainam-overseas-private-limited_hiring-merchandiseplanning-demandplanning-activity-7460939751706013697-rK9v
6. Arihant Finova (company-owned fashion stores across India incl. Nashik, Nagpur; diversified parent). Size unverified. Role: Head of Retail Operations / Planning and Inventory Manager. Source: https://arihantfinova.com/
7. Chitralekha Group (multi-store apparel retail; store facades in multiple cities, e.g. Muzaffarpur). HQ and size unverified. Role: Head of Retail / Merchandise Planning Manager. Source: https://chitralekha.net/about.html
8. Derby (South India, Chennai cited; menswear; 65+ exclusive stores of 600-1,000 sq ft). Employee count unverified. Role: Head of Retail Operations / Merchandise Planning Manager. Source: https://fortunefranchise.in/derby/
9. Knya (medical apparel D2C moving offline; new stores in Tirupati and Indore). Size unverified; partly verified (no direct company URL captured). Role: Head of Supply Chain / Retail Expansion Lead.
10. Ashiesh Nx Pvt Ltd (Nagpur; multi-branch retail apparel). 12 employees reported, below the 20-250 band, low priority. Source: https://linkedin.com/company/ashiesh-nx

## Reply-handling guide
- Interested: reply promptly, offer a short intro call, do not imply a pilot is agreed until scope and fit are discussed.
  "Thanks, {first_name}, glad to hear this may be relevant. Could we set up a short call to understand your current process, data, and what a useful scoped pilot might look like? What times work for you?"
- Not now: respect timing, ask when to follow up, record the preference.
  "Understood, thanks for letting me know. Would you prefer that I check back around {month}, or should I leave it with you?"
- Wrong person: thank them, ask for the right role only if appropriate, stop pitching them.
  "Thanks for the correction. Is there a role or team at {company} that owns {planning_area}? No worries if you'd rather not redirect me."
- Unsubscribe / objection: confirm, suppress the address, stop the sequence immediately, do not ask for a reason.
  "Understood. I've noted your request and won't send further marketing emails. Thank you for letting me know."

## Reconciled recommendation (the CGO review wins where it disagrees with 01 to 05)
1. One vertical for 30 days: Apparel. Park Hardware (file 07 and the 50 Hardware prospects).
2. Define "signed" as a one-page 90-day pilot letter: free or deferred fee, price locked, data access, weekly 30-minute call, case-study rights.
3. No price in any external message. Remove it from the SDR script (file 04).
4. Founder-led, warm and referral-first outreach (LinkedIn and WhatsApp), with email as support.
5. 40 hand-qualified targets, not 150, each passing a 4-signal gate: right vertical, verified size, named decision-maker, confirmed SKU-level data.
6. Two touches, not three. Plan on a 1 to 3% total reply rate, not 12%.

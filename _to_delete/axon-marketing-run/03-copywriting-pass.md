## 4) Phrases to avoid
Untrue (we cannot say these yet):
- "Trusted by leading brands", "used by X retailers", any logo or reference we do not have
- Any accuracy figure ("95% forecast accuracy", "improves accuracy by X%")
- Any savings or outcome claim ("reduce stockouts by 30%", "cut dead stock", "payback in 3 months")
- "Proven", "battle-tested", "case study available"
- Compliance or security claims ("ISO certified", "SOC 2", "GDPR compliant", "enterprise-grade security")
- "AI-powered" as a standalone boast; say "probabilistic forecasts" and "stockout-aware demand correction"
- "Partners with" any named tool until an integration ships
- "Only Rs 14,995" / "just Rs 14,995": state as indicative pricing, plainly, and only if asked

Spammy:
- Fake "Re:" or "Fwd:" prefixes (note: file 02 Day 5 subject A uses "Re:", change it)
- "Dear Sir/Madam", "I hope this email finds you well", "Quick question", "Just bumping this"
- "Act now", "limited time", "exclusive invitation", fake scarcity
- "Free trial" when no self-serve trial exists; call a scoped pilot a pilot
- "Industry-leading", "best-in-class", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary", "game-changing", "world-class", "disrupting", "no-brainer"
- "Guaranteed ROI", "risk-free"
- "Circling back", "touching base", "reaching out" as the whole reason for the email

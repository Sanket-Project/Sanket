# Company-level prospect research: Hardware (India), pass 2
Author: Research Analyst (Axon, Research & Insights), model DeepSeek Flash, run 2026-10-09.
Method: Composio's Exa-backed web search, read-only. Nothing sent, no CRM or HubSpot writes, no accounts or forms. Company-level only; no individuals named.
Scope caveat: the analyst read hand-tools results and the start of the Ludhiana fasteners results from pass 1, plus 10 new verification queries. It did NOT read these buckets: electrical fittings/switchgear, building hardware, multi-store hardware retail (Rajkot), sanitary ware/plumbing distribution, industrial tools (Pune), and hardware inventory/purchase-hiring posts. Those buckets are saved in the analyst's sandbox (/mnt/files/web_b1.json) and need a third pass.
Employee counts are verified for only two companies (Taparia 288, Magadh about 9-11). Ranking below is on documented channel breadth and SKU complexity, not verified size. The apparel list (file 06) is the stronger one.

## Companies found
1. Osho Tools Private Limited. City unverified. Hand tools plus auto parts and tractor components; founded 1996; documented 800+ dealership network. Why: three product families and a wide dealer network mean both factory-side and dealer-side forecasting. Size unverified. Role: Head of Supply Chain / Distribution Planning Manager. Source: https://linkedin.com/company/osho-tools-private-limited
2. Eastman Cast & Forge Ltd (Ludhiana, Punjab). Hand tools and forged/cast products; nationwide distributor network and public dealership-enquiry process. Why: long batch lead times against dealer-level demand. Size unverified, has a separate OEM division so it may exceed 250: verify. Role: Head of Supply Chain / Channel and Distribution Planning Manager. Source: https://eastmanoem.com/about-us/ (site https://eastmanhandtools.com/)
3. Bhavya Industries (Ludhiana). Industrial fasteners (high-tensile bolts, nuts, washers, threaded rods, structural) for automotive, construction, railways. Why: hundreds of SKUs by thread, length, grade, finish, standard; many small-batch demand streams. Size unverified. Role: Head of Sales and Distribution / Purchase and Inventory Manager. Source: https://bhavyaindustriesldh.com/
4. Nexo Industries (Ludhiana). High-tensile fasteners, domestic plus export. Size unverified. Role: Head of Supply Chain / Sales and Distribution Manager. Source: https://www.nexoindia.com/
5. Nexo World (Ludhiana). Possibly the same business group as Nexo Industries: treat as one relationship, not two prospects. Source: http://www.nexoworld.in/
6. Babu Exports (Ludhiana). Fasteners with export activity; export and domestic channels with different lead times on the same SKU base. Size unverified. Role: Head of Operations / Purchase and Inventory Manager. Source: https://www.babuexports.com/
7. Jon Bhandari Tools (city unverified). Hand/industrial tools; nationwide distributor network and a public dealership-enquiry route. Size unverified. Role: Head of Sales and Distribution / Inventory Planning Manager. Source: https://jonbhandaritools.net/
8. Alok Tools (Alok International Pvt. Ltd., city unverified). Hand and industrial tools manufacturer and supplier; no evidence found of a named in-house planning function. Size unverified. Role: Head of Supply Chain / Inventory Planning Manager. Source: https://www.aloktools.com/
9. Magadh Hand Tools (Jaipur, Rajasthan; also New Delhi and Bengaluru). Industrial hand tools since 1979; dealer network of roughly 300-600 dealers. About 9-11 employees, BELOW the 20-250 band: low priority. Role: Head of Sales and Distribution. Source: https://linkedin.com/company/magadh-hand-tools
10. Taparia Tools Limited (Nashik, Maharashtra; unit in Goa). Precision hand tools; 800+ distributors. 288 employees and revenue Rs 1,03,721.96 lakh in FY2025-26 per its annual report; BSE-listed and large. Exclude from first outreach. Source: https://tapariatools.com/about_us.html

Also surfaced in the Ludhiana fastener cluster with only name and city (not vetted): Optimum International, Ravina Fasteners, Keshav Enterprises (wholesaler), Rudra Fasteners (wholesaler), NSB Group (wholesaler/supplier).

## Analyst's top five (hardware)
1. Osho Tools  2. Eastman Cast & Forge  3. Bhavya Industries  4. Jon Bhandari Tools  5. Nexo Industries (with Nexo World as the same group)
Excluded by design: Taparia Tools (too large and listed), Magadh Hand Tools (too small).

## Honest read
These are manufacturers and channel-led businesses more than "distributors and regional retail chains", which was the ICP in file 01. If SANKET wants the original Hardware ICP (distributors, retail chains, 20-250 employees), the third research pass should target the unread buckets above, and the ICP wording should probably widen to "hardware manufacturers with dealer networks". That is a decision for the founder.

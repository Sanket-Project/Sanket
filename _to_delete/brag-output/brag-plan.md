# Brag Plan: SANKET

## What is this app?
SANKET is an enterprise-grade AI predictive analytics and supply chain operating system that unifies sales, inventory, and market signals to forecast every SKU and automate operational decisions across Fashion, Electronics, Pharma, and AgTech.

## The angle
High-end startup launch and demo-day showcase. Global supply chains bleed billions on spreadsheet guesswork, leading to catastrophic stockouts and dead-stock markdowns. SANKET replaces intuition with an auditable, multi-model AI forecasting engine that turns predictions into approved, real-world replenishment decisions.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A cinematic dark aperture reveals fragmented red spreadsheet cells flashing "STOCKOUT: SKU-8820" and "4 DAYS OF COVER", shattered by the question: *"Why are multi-billion dollar supply chains still guessing?"*

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The SANKET Core Reveal**: Glowing time-series envelope showing empirical p10/p50/p90 confidence bands replacing flat guess-lines.
- **The 4-Step Operational Loop**: `01 INGEST` → `02 FORECAST` → `03 DECIDE` → `04 ACT` transitioning with buttery precision.
- **Per-SKU Multi-Model Routing**: Amazon Chronos zero-shot for new items without history, stacked with TFT, N-HiTS, and gradient boosted trees.
- **Real UI Decision Layer**: Real product screenshot with high-fidelity UI overlays showing automated PO recommendations, days-of-cover risk, and multi-vertical intelligence (Fashion, Electronics, Pharma GxP 21 CFR Part 11).

## Outro / punchline
The SANKET logo snaps into center focus with laser clarity, flanked by the bold headline: *"Stop guessing demand. Forecast every SKU."*

## User flow worth showing
`01 INGEST` (Connect Shopify, PostgreSQL, ERPs) → `02 FORECAST` (Zero-shot foundation models + per-SKU ensembles with p10/p50/p90 confidence intervals) → `03 DECIDE` (Ranked operational actions: 4.0 days of cover alert on SKU-8820) → `04 ACT` (One-click reorder approval with immutable GxP audit log).
